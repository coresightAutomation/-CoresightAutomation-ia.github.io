About CoreSight

Precision Data Support for Research Teams

At CoreSight, we help market research businesses streamline their reporting, data processing, and quality checks with precision and efficiency. Our goal is simple — reduce operational burden so your team can focus on high-impact work that drives growth and revenue.

We specialize in reliable manual reporting support along with smart automation solutions, including VBA-based tools that enhance accuracy and turnaround time. By combining practical expertise with a strong understanding of survey data workflows, we ensure consistent, high-quality output at a cost-effective scale.

Whether handling large volumes of survey data, ensuring quality control, or optimizing reporting pipelines — we act as an extended arm of your team.
